External Modules may have settings that include usernames. When a username changes and setting(s) in the external modules utilize a username, it may mean the external module breaks and updating the username will fix it.

Running this script to find mentions of "user" in an external module.

Use your knowledge of the external modules on your system. The results are a rough guess and by no means exhaustive.

SELECT `project_id`, `key` FROM redcap_external_module_settings WHERE `key` LIKE '%user%' ORDER BY `project_id`, `external_module_id`;
